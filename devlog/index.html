31 July 2026 · World reconstruction

The first reconstructed world

One original save now drives the model, asteroid surface and strategic map.

The importer reads typed asteroid, faction, building, ship, personnel and diplomacy records while preserving unknown bytes. Reference colonies come from original relationships, not demo data.

Terrain relief, construction masks and the strategic map are projections of that same world. Rendering and hit testing share recovered geometry.

Confirmed result

Map origin and cell pitch match the reference; zoomed asteroid centres differ by at most one pixel.

16 July 2026 · Foundations

Foundations of the reconstruction

The project starts with evidence, legal asset import and strict boundaries between game state and presentation.

Supplied original-game captures document visible states; executable code, assets and saves cover hidden behaviour. The domain model stays independent of files, graphics and input.

Screens use MVP: models hold state, presenters handle actions and views remain replaceable. Assets come from a legal installation and are not distributed with OpenFA.

OpenFA loads standard declarative content from a versioned .ofapkg archive. Extensions are data-only and ordered by stable priority and ID.

AI helps navigate the decompilation. Tests replay asset, save and simulation cases; generated explanations remain hypotheses.

Working rule

A screenshot can identify a target. Behaviour needs code, data, save or runtime evidence.
