Refined Terran models · before / after
Why these models were created

These models grew out of Google rejecting OpenFA from the Play Store for “instability”. At the time, OpenFA depended entirely on Fragile Allegiance data and had no assets of its own, so the game could not start during pre-launch testing. With AI assistance, original-inspired 3D assets were created that could serve as an alternative graphics set in the future.

These are source art studies; use as an alternative graphics set remains future integration work.
